# ABI marketplace visual direction

Status: visual world confirmed by the user; composition approval pending.

## Brand and references
Familiar marketplace conventions with Shopify storefront clarity, Amazon search/category usefulness, Etsy product discovery. No eBay reference. Retain ABI name and its warm beige identity. The latest user clarification makes beige the dominant canvas and forest green a restrained accent from Open Exchange. Buyers and sellers share equal strategic priority.

## Tokens
- Canvas: #f4ede2; primary surface: #fffdf8; subtle surface: #e9e0d2.
- Ink: #292820; brand accent: #123b30; secondary ink: #625e52.
- Primary action: #185c43 with white text; accent: #dafa70 with forest text.
- Dividers: #d8cebe.
- Typography: IBM Plex Sans for UI and display, existing self-hosted family; clear weight hierarchy, no serif display or monospace decoration.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 px.
- Corners: 8 px for inputs/buttons, 12 px product image panels; avoid nested cards and decorative shadows.

## Composition grammar
Search prominent in header, straightforward category navigation, compact commerce headline, image-led product grid, clear listing metadata. Avoid a giant marketing hero that pushes browsing below the fold. Seller entry visible in main navigation and a substantial seller section. Software, digital services and physical goods share coherent product presentation with accurate type labels.

## Imagery
High-quality product photography for physical examples and thoughtful software cover art. No financial charts, crypto imagery, invented vendor endorsements or availability claims. Concept illustrations explicitly use sample listings.

## Interaction and states
Readable search and category filters; keyboard-visible focus; product cards with explicit action names; semantic forms; responsive grids; reduced-motion support. Preserve API/auth/approval contracts. Physical checkout and fulfillment are not implied by illustrative imagery.

## Scope
All public routes and shared chrome; buyer console views; seller console views; account, legal, error and empty states. Route coverage is tracked in .impeccable/coverage-plan.md.
